## 1. Getting Started

### Account Registration

To use the Pixie AI platform, you first need to register an account.

1.  Navigate to the registration page.
2.  Fill in your email address, a strong password, and confirm your password.
3.  Complete any CAPTCHA verification.
4.  Click "Register".
5.  You will receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to activate your account.

### Logging In

Once registered and verified, you can log in.

1.  Navigate to the login page.
2.  Enter your registered email address and password.
3.  Complete any CAPTCHA verification.
4.  Click "Login".

### Password Recovery

If you forget your password, you can recover it.

1.  On the login page, click "Forgot Password".
2.  Enter your registered email address.
3.  Complete any CAPTCHA verification.
4.  An email with a password reset link will be sent to you.
5.  Click the link in the email and follow the instructions to set a new password.

## 2. P2P Video Chat

P2P (Peer-to-Peer) video chat allows you to have private video conversations with another user.

### Initiating a P2P Chat

1.  Log in to your account.
2.  Navigate to the P2P chat section (e.g., a user list or a "Start P2P" button).
3.  Select the user you wish to chat with.
4.  Choose whether the session should be public or private (with a password).
5.  Click "Start P2P Chat". A unique link will be generated for your session.
6.  Share this link with the other participant.

### Joining a P2P Chat

1.  Receive a P2P chat link from another user.
2.  If the session is private, you will be prompted to enter a password.
3.  Click the link to join the session.
4.  Ensure your microphone and camera are enabled.

### Managing Your P2P Chat

*   **Mute/Unmute Audio**: Click the microphone icon to toggle your audio.
*   **Enable/Disable Video**: Click the camera icon to toggle your video.
*   **Leave Chat**: Click the "Leave" button to exit the session.

## 3. Broadcast Livestreaming

Broadcast livestreaming allows you to stream video and audio to a larger audience.

### Creating a Livestream

1.  Log in to your account with a "broadcaster" role.
2.  Navigate to the livestream creation section (e.g., "Go Live" panel).
3.  Enter a title and optional description for your stream.
4.  Choose whether the stream should be public or private (with a password).
5.  Click "Start Livestream". A unique link will be generated for your stream.
6.  Share this link with your audience.

### Managing Your Livestream

*   **Start/Stop Stream**: Use the controls in the "Go Live" panel to start or stop your broadcast.
*   **Mute/Unmute Audio**: Toggle your microphone.
*   **Enable/Disable Video**: Toggle your camera.
*   **Viewer Count**: Monitor the number of active viewers.
*   **Stream Settings**: Adjust video quality, audio bitrate, etc.

### Viewing a Livestream

1.  Receive a livestream link from a broadcaster.
2.  If the stream is private, you will be prompted to enter a password.
3.  Click the link to join the stream.
4.  You will be able to see and hear the broadcaster. Your audio and video inputs will be disabled.

## 4. Troubleshooting

*   **Cannot connect to a session**:
    *   Check your internet connection.
    *   Ensure the session link is correct and not expired.
    *   If it's a private session, verify the password.
    *   Contact the session host if the issue persists.
*   **Poor audio/video quality**:
    *   Check your internet bandwidth.
    *   Close other applications that might be using bandwidth.
    *   Ensure your microphone and camera are working correctly.
*   **Cannot create a broadcast**:
    *   Ensure your account has the "broadcaster" role.
    *   Check your internet connection.

For further assistance, please contact support.
